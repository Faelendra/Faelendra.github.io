The Story

The Name

Faelendra is the name the work answers to when it prefers not to be hurried... otherwise called Brenda.

It is less a disguise than a doorway: the name belonging to the part of the maker that follows curiosity first, works slowly when the work asks for it, and is perfectly willing to change direction when something interesting appears.

The Making

Wood and turning. Earth and clay. Paint and ink. Found things with stories.

Faelendra is not particularly interested in choosing only one craft. Some pieces begin with timber, some with paint, some with an object rescued because it was far too interesting to leave behind.

Curiosity decides what happens next.

The Why

Things made slowly. Things with character. Imperfections that prove hands were involved.

The point is not perfection for its own sake. It is the pleasure of taking raw material, an unfinished thought, or an overlooked object and discovering what it might become.

Creating because making something where there wasn't something before is its own peculiar kind of magic.
